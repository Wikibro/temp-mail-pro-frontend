What Is a Virtual Card?

A virtual card is a card number provided digitally for online payments. Depending on the issuer, it may offer controls such as spending limits, separate card profiles, or a different billing setup from your everyday bank card. Availability, fees, verification, funding, and merchant acceptance vary by provider and region.

A virtual card should not be treated as a way to defeat fraud checks or a guarantee that a platform will approve a signup. Payment processors can still reject cards based on merchant policy, region, account history, billing details, or risk controls.

PST.NET Features to Review

PST.NET's published materials describe several features that may be relevant to advertisers, subscription testers, and teams managing multiple payment workflows. Offers, eligibility, card types, and limits can change, so confirm each item on the provider's current pricing and product pages.

Advertising Cashback

Some PST.NET materials advertise up to 3% cashback on eligible advertising spend. Treat this as a plan or campaign benefit rather than a universal rate: check qualifying ad platforms, spend thresholds, payout timing, exclusions, and whether the offer is available in your region.

Card Issuance and Team Scale

PST.NET may offer high-volume or unlimited card issuance on selected plans or account types. “Unlimited” should not be read as unlimited spending or unrestricted merchant acceptance. Review account limits, funding rules, review triggers, and any fair-use conditions before creating cards for a team or test environment.

Some advertised private or enterprise perks may include up to 100 free virtual cards. Confirm whether the benefit is monthly, promotional, plan-specific, or subject to approval before relying on it in a budget or campaign workflow.

PST Pulse BIN Checker

The PST Pulse BIN checker is described as a tool for reviewing BIN-related information before attempting a payment. A BIN check can help with planning, but it cannot guarantee approval: the merchant, issuer, billing details, location, account history, and real-time risk systems can still affect the result.

Fees and Card Controls

PST.NET may advertise 0% fees for selected transaction or decline scenarios. Always verify the current fee schedule, because issuance, funding, foreign exchange, refunds, withdrawals, inactivity, and other account actions may have separate terms. Look for available controls such as card limits, spending visibility, and team permissions when comparing plans.

3D Secure and BIN Categories

Review the provider's current documentation for 3D Secure support and the card types available to your account. Marketing terms such as premium, credit-status, or high-tier BIN can describe a product category, but they do not guarantee that a card will be accepted by a particular trial, ad platform, or merchant.

Compliance and Account Security

PST.NET's published materials describe Sumsub-based eKYC and AML monitoring for account or transaction review. That is a compliance and risk-control process, not a promise of anonymity or universal approval. You may be asked to provide information during onboarding or later reviews, depending on the product, region, funding activity, and applicable requirements.

Use accurate account and billing information, keep your login protected, and follow both PST.NET's acceptable-use rules and the payment platform's advertising or subscription policies.

What to Check Before Choosing a Provider

• **Current fees:** Check issuance, funding, foreign-exchange, inactivity, and failed-payment fees.

• **Verification policy:** Requirements may vary by country, product, transaction, or risk review.

• **Merchant acceptance:** Some trial services and ad platforms restrict prepaid or virtual cards.

• **Billing accuracy:** Use billing details that are permitted and accurate for the transaction.

• **Support and disputes:** Understand how refunds, disputes, card replacement, and account closure work.

• **Account security:** Use a strong password and enable available security controls.
