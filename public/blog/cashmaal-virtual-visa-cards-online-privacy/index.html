What to Verify Before You Sign Up

Verification and Eligibility

Do not assume that a product has no identity or verification requirements. Financial services can request additional information based on location, product type, funding source, transaction pattern, or compliance obligations. Check the current policy that applies to your account.

Fees and Funding

Compare card issuance, reload, currency conversion, inactivity, withdrawal, and failed-payment fees. Also check supported funding methods, settlement times, refund handling, and whether a merchant can place a temporary authorization hold.

Billing Information and AVS

Address Verification Service checks can compare billing details with information held by a payment provider. Use only billing information that is accurate, permitted, and accepted by the card issuer and merchant. Never rely on a virtual card to misrepresent your identity, location, or billing address.

Merchant and Regional Acceptance

A prepaid or virtual Visa card may not be accepted by every subscription, marketplace, ad platform, or trial service. Confirm regional availability and merchant restrictions before depending on a card for an important purchase.

When a Virtual Card May Help

A virtual card may be useful when you want to limit exposure of an everyday payment card for a legitimate online purchase, test checkout behavior in an approved environment, or separate business and personal payment workflows.
